Our Services

A closer look at how each of our six services works.

01 — PET Recycling

Sorting and reprocessing plastic bottles, sacks and nylon waste at scale, one tonne every hour.

PET bottles, sacks and nylon film are collected, sorted by polymer type, washed and processed through our extrusion line into clean granules at a rate of one tonne every hour — raw input for a new generation of plastic products.

1 tonne processed every hour
02 — Egg Crate Manufacturing

Producing egg crates from recovered pulp, twelve bundles every hour.

Recovered paper and cardboard pulp is molded into egg crates on our production line, turning waste fibre into a product used daily by poultry farms and food distributors across the region — at a rate of twelve bundles every hour.

12 bundles produced every hour
03 — Organic Manure

Converting organic by-products into manure that restores farmland.

Organic waste and agricultural by-products are processed into nutrient-rich manure, returning value to the soil instead of the landfill. Watch the process below, from collection to finished product.

Restoring farmland, one batch at a time
04 — Cotton Recycling

Recovered cotton reworked into pillows, cushions and teddy bears.

Cotton waste from textile and garment offcuts is cleaned, carded and reworked into soft filling for pillows, cushions and teddy bears — giving fibre that would otherwise be discarded a second, tangible life.

Textile waste, reworked by hand
05 — Agro Allied

Livestock, farming and allied agricultural services that close the loop.

Our agro allied operations connect recycled inputs — organic manure among them — to livestock and crop farming, closing the loop between what we recover and what the land needs to stay productive.

Recycled inputs, productive land

06 — Carton Recycling

Collecting and compressing cartons for re-entry into the paper supply chain.

Used cartons are collected, sorted and baled for re-entry into the paper supply chain — one of the simplest, highest-volume ways we divert material from landfill every single day.
